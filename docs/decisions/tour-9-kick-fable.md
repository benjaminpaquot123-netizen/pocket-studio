# Tour 9, lot kick-fable (Benjamin, 2026-11-25: « le son est pur mais ça manque de volume et le kick tape pas assez », version B)

- The studio kick reads its sweep from the voice (PITCH_DROP = start over the note in semitones, DROP_TIME = time constant), with the old values as DrumKit defaults (26 st ≈ x4.5 over 35 ms; hard 31 st ≈ x6 over 45 ms). Reason: one engine, per-family settings, saved projects sound as before. Back: StudioKick ignores s.drop / s.dropTime again.
- Drive range widened: gain 0.8 + 3.7 x drive (was 1.5 + 2.5 x drive; same gain 3 at the default 0.6). Reason: house, trance and drum & bass need a nearly clean kick (gain 1.2 at 0.1), disco and hard a dirtier one. Back: the old formula.
- Three old studio_kicks checks rewritten with their reason: 80-160 Hz measured on the tail only (the slower sweep sits above 80 Hz in its first 100 ms by design), click share > -35 dB (was -25), click layer measured as the difference with / without click (the saturated rise is broadband now).
- The per-style table lives in StudioDrums.kt (KICK_FAMILIES, by style id), not in the recipes' JSON. Reason: it covers every style in one place and touches no recipe file of the other lots. Back: kickVoice without the table.
- Every style now puts a studio kick on its kick pad (before: House, Techno and the 808 styles only; the others played the kit v3's default kick at 52 Hz). Reason: Benjamin's table names a kick for each family. Back: return null outside House/Techno/808 again.
- The kick stays in the key: the root or fifth nearest to the family's note (within 3.5 semitones), the 808 styles keep kickHz (report 12: the 808's key). Reason: a fixed Hz would beat against the key's bass. Back: tune = the family's Hz.
- Drum & bass keeps DNB_KICK_DECAY / DNB_KICK_CLICK so « Kit ▾ › Drum & bass » still recognises its kick.
- Point (c), grave tenu + effacement à chaque kick: the engine already has it (the pump: recipes' mix.pump, the « pump » preset, PUMP_808, RUMBLE_STRIP; the kick's own hold via « sub »).
